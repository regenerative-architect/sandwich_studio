Private notebook controls

Checking available on-device storage…

Privacy and reliability: No accounts, network requests, advertising, or customer data are required. Browser storage can be cleared by the device or browser; a shared computer is not private. An exported backup is an ordinary file—keep it somewhere safe and do not publish it. Import replaces this notebook after confirmation.
How to work fully offline

Save this single file as index.html and open it in a browser. It has no external scripts, fonts, or services. Saving the file is different from saving your notebook: use JSON export to transfer entries. Browser restrictions on local files may prevent persistent storage, in which case export during the session.

How this studio was designed

Financial model: Cents are used for recorded money. Ingredient usage is multiplied by stored per-unit cost at the time of a sale; resulting cost is rounded to cents per transaction. Inventory has a weighted-average cost after restocking. Manual count corrections can change future estimates but not past sale costs. Profit and cash movement are intentionally different.

Food protection: No home recipe, allergen badge, thermometer input, or checklist can verify safe food. Adults must validate the actual products, environmental temperature, preparation area and current local rules. For children, no unsupervised knives, raw-meat prep, stranger contact or online payment.

Constraints: No internet requirement, no credentials, no payment processing, no child profiles, no personal-performance ranking, no autonomous legal guidance, no unverifiable impact claims. Learning stars come only from optional educational activities.
